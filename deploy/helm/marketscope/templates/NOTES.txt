   METRICS_BEARER_TOKEN (when metrics are enabled).

2. The pre-upgrade migration Job runs `alembic upgrade head`. Review its logs:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/{{ include "marketscope.fullname" . }}-migrate-{{ .Release.Revision }}

3. Verify readiness:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods
   curl https://<your-host>/health/ready

4. Roll back with `helm rollback {{ .Release.Name }} <revision>`.
   Helm does not reverse Alembic migrations; see docs/operations-runbook.md
   before rolling back across a schema boundary.
